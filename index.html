01 / Overview

Trust must begin at the signal.

AI-driven systems increasingly transform speech, audio-visual media, sensor streams, source code, and other structured data into consequential decisions and actions. Their expanding autonomy also enlarges the attack surface: synthetic media undermines authenticity, corrupted observations trigger hallucinations, malicious instructions compromise tool use, hidden triggers alter agent behavior, and identity leakage threatens privacy.

These challenges share a signal-processing foundation. Trustworthy decisions depend on how models represent, align, filter, validate, and act on information under noise, manipulation, distribution shift, and adversarial control.

Why this session

One forum for an increasingly connected threat surface.

The session brings together questions that are usually handled in isolation, creating a shared language for robust methods and reproducible evaluation.
